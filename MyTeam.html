Software Development Team Roles and Responsibilities

Explain the key roles and responsibilities within your software development team. Use the examples below if applicable, or add your own.

My Role

In my role I am responsible for several tasks:

  • Development - I am responsible for several aspects of development in the Relish system and often any maintenace that needs to take place within the scripts to fix bugs.
  • Server Management - I am responsible for monitoring activity on the servers in order to ensure that they are maintained at a reasonable level, in case any further action needs to be taken.
  • Recipe Management - Although not strictly related to IT, I am the main individual responsible for managing the recipes in the system.
  • IT Support - I provide IT support to both internal and external customers within Relish in order to assist with their problems.

Richard - IT Director

Richard oversees all IT related business and directs and manages any projects and maintenance of the system. He is a vital member of the team as he is the leader and organises how the team operates. In addition, due to his experience he often acts as a mentor for all in the team as he knows how to solve almost any problem that may arise.

Curtis - Lead PHP Developer

Curtis is responsible for the majority of development of the Relish system which allows for the constant improvement of the platform. In my role, I support much of the work he does and often ask him for guidance in my work.

Adele - Data Manager

Adele spends most of her working day carrying out data-driven investigations and is responsible for most of the integrations between Relish, the schools, and the payment providers Relish interact with and this allows Relish to grow as a company. As one of the most senior members of the team, I often go to Adele if I have any issues in these areas.
